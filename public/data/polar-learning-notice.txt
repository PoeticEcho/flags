Final polar expansion, 2026-10-02

The original 197-country education edition remains unchanged. Greenland (GL)
is a self-governing territory within the Kingdom of Denmark. Antarctica (AQ)
is a continent, not a country, and has no capital or permanent resident population.
The map uses a Mercator projection, so land beyond about 85 degrees latitude
is clipped; the South Pole itself is not shown. The Antarctic pin represents
King Sejong research station, not a capital. Its position is supplied by KOPRI.

Greenland population: Statistics Greenland, Central Population Register,
1 July 2025, 56,831 registered residents. This differs in source and method
from the UN WPP projections retained for the original 197 countries.
https://stat.gl/publ/en/BE/202504/contents/Midyear%20Population.htm
Nuuk coordinate: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0 attribution, representative city location.
https://www.geonames.org/search.html?country=GL&q=Nuuk
https://www.geonames.org/about.html
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Country/territory SVG colouring templates: lipis/flag-icons 7.5.0, MIT;
the existing country-data-license and flag SVG source notices still apply.
All three supplied flag PNG files retain exact uploaded bytes and hashes.
The Antarctic silhouette is an unofficial symbol, not an official national flag.
The polar-grid design is the Antarctic Treaty emblem. Its inclusion as a
user-provided learning reference does not claim Antarctic Treaty endorsement.
https://www.ats.aq/devAS/Meetings/Measure?id=289&lang=e

Two new postcard scenes were generated with built-in image_gen as stylized
educational illustrations, not photographs. Original PNG files, exact prompts,
sources and review notes are included in the downloadable update bundle.
The Greenland landscape is generalized rather than a verified exact viewpoint.
Primary-source factual anchors and original observation activities are listed
in polar-destinations.json. Summaries are newly written, not copied articles.
Automated checks and image inspection are not a full browser/touch/GPU audit.
