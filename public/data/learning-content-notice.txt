Little World Explorers — 197-country five-story education edition, 2026-10-02

The bilingual educational paragraphs are original child-oriented summaries of
facts, not copies of complete third-party articles. Country-specific primary
references and the expansion review scope are in learning-story-expansion-sources.json.
This edition preserves all 591 bilingual stories and 1,970 keyword pairs from
the three-story edition, then adds two bilingual passages per country: 394
additional pairs, 985 total, five per country. New passages include original
explanations and guided comparisons, observations and diagram activities.
Primary references support factual anchors; authored teaching activities are
not quoted source statements. Country/capital comparisons reuse the app's
existing roster. This expansion is not an independent all-capital coordinate audit.
The earlier 137-country enrichment is recorded in learning-enrichment-sources.json.

Country names, borders, languages and related baseline database material:

The original baseline attribution and license notice remain at
/data/country-data-license.txt and the baseline at /data/learning-basics.json.

Heritage fact references: UNESCO World Heritage Centre and UNESCO Intangible
Cultural Heritage. UNESCO WHC descriptions identify CC BY-SA IGO 3.0 licensing:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/igo/
UNESCO is credited as the source of the referenced heritage information;
it does not endorse this app. Tentative nominations are not inscriptions.
Other primary sources include official governments, museums, conservation
project organisations and national tourism bodies, linked per country.
No third-party webpage images are included in this education ZIP.

Population: United Nations, World Population Prospects 2024, Medium variant,
1 July 2025 projection (GEN/01/REV1), CC BY 3.0 IGO.
https://population.un.org/wpp/
These values are projections, not 2026 live counts or censuses.

Postcard captions identify scenes separately from capital locations.
The education ZIP contains captions, not postcard image files or app binaries.
